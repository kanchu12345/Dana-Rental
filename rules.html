RENTAL RULES & TERMS

Please review our simple guidelines for a safe and hassle-free driving experience in Sri Lanka.

1. Required Documents

  • For Foreign Tourists: Valid Passport, National Driving License from home country, and International Driving Permit (IDP) or Sri Lanka Driving Permit endorsement (AAC).
  • For Sri Lankan Citizens: National Identity Card (NIC) / Driving License and proof of address.

2. Mileage & 200 Free KM Policy

Every 24-hour rental period comes with 200 free kilometers included. Excess mileage beyond the cumulative free limit is calculated upon vehicle return at standard transparent rates (e.g. Rs. 40-70 per extra km depending on vehicle class).

3. Security Deposit & Payments

A refundable cash / card security deposit is collected during vehicle handover (Rs. 5,000 for Scooters, Rs. 15,000 for Cars). The deposit is refunded immediately upon returning the vehicle in good condition.

4. Safety & Helmets

All scooter and motorbike rentals include 2 clean helmets free of charge. Wearing helmets is mandatory by Sri Lankan traffic law for both rider and pillion passenger.

5. Fuel Policy

Vehicles are handed over with sufficient fuel to reach the nearest gas station. Please return the vehicle with the same level of fuel as at pickup.
